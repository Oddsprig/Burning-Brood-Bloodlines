# Arms trade and arms contracts (Douglas picked "Yes, all of it", 2026-10-04 ~22:23)
"It's important because as factions lose people they lose weapons and armor a lot of the time."
- Cross-race/faction trade of weapons and armour (and bows/arrows) on the existing caravans and supply-target prices; diplomacy gates it (war: no trade; hostile: embargo; trade pact: boosted).
- Maker's marks: items remember maker family/business, town and race (and style via template culture); shown on the item; foreign gear usable by anyone through normal skills, no race penalty.
- Armory-driven arms contracts: any faction whose armory is below its supply target for ANY weapon/armour item offers to buy a contract from any faction at peace with a surplus of that item; agreed between faction heads via diplomacy; delivered in batches by real caravans over a period; kept deliveries improve relations, broken deals damage them; escort/raid jobs for the company (raids paid by rivals).
- The loop must be real: gear lost with the dead (looted, scrapped, left on the field) lowers forces' kit and the armory vs its targets; local smiths/bowyers refill first; contracts and trade cover the rest.
