WORLD AND FACTION VIEWS PART 2 of 2. Same rules as part 1 and the town screen spec (every number from the sim, sort any column, tap anything to open it).

On the move
- Every force and convoy on the map: caravans, arms convoys, AI armies, militias, war bands, monster packs, the player's company. Row: kind, owner (faction/family), from -> to, position (road segment), men, cargo or strength, supply level, status (marching / hunting / shadowing / fleeing / camped). Filter by kind and faction.
- Counts: caravans on the road, robbed this season, armies in the field, interceptions this season.
- Header (reference On the move, Douglas 01:20): consignments on the way, their worth, by carrier kind (wagon, pack animal, porter, boat: whatever the sim really has) with carrier counts, carriers going home empty, relief grain on the way, fetched by the buyer vs sent by the seller.
- What's on the way, by good: consignments, units, loads, worth.
- Arriving soonest (first 60): from -> to, what (relief marked), by what, left, arrives. Tap -> the caravan and its crew.

Goods (reference Rice & grain; fewer goods for phone)
- The 6 categories (Food, Drink, Raw, Made, Metal, Arms) with world stock, days of use and price spread. Tap a category -> goods; tap a good -> row per town: stock, days, price, x base, 7-day change. Cheapest and dearest town marked, so trade routes are visible.

Food flows (reference Rice & grain; Douglas 01:20)
- Header: how the world feeds itself across climates (cold north / temperate / hot south): which regions are short and which have surplus in grain, meat, fruit, beer, wine, in days of food for one person.
- Flows now: food going north / south / east / west between regions, days' food and consignments each way. Rows: way, from, to, by what, quantity, arrives.
- The great food houses: the 3 merchant families shipping the most food. Each: shipped this year, a day lately vs long run, bought from families this year, held in their granaries of capacity, and their books (day, what, quantity, coins). Tap -> family.
- Relief grain: granary grain sent to starving towns, by whom.

Fights (reference "The county's fights"; Douglas 01:21: a world log of fights)
- Summary line: fights this season seen on the map vs resolved out of sight vs called off; dead, wounded, maimed, died of wounds later, fled. (Taken prisoner / ransom only if the sim has captives; otherwise "not tracked".)
- Coming up: fights already set by the AI (raid, war interception, sweep of a bandit camp, monster lair hunt), with day and hour, place, each side and its numbers.
- The log, newest first: one plain sentence per real engagement, built from the real result, e.g. "Late Autumn d12: the Black Ridge band came down on the Harrow farms (Ashford) at first light and drove off its men (one killed, six wounded), took 71 days' grain and 43 coins; they hold the road for three days." Covers AI clashes, war interceptions, caravan raids, monster attacks, militia turnouts, orc succession fights and the player's own battles.
- Tap a fight -> both sides (forces and named persons), dead and wounded by name (tap -> person), loot taken, what changed after (road held, relation drop, town lost grain).
- Filter: kind, faction, town, player involved. Town and faction views show the same log filtered to them.

Mines & land (reference The hills)
- Ore deposits: town, ore, richness, mine (tap business), output per day, workers.
- World land: acres by crop, pasture, woodland (and woodland cut vs regrown per season), herds by animal.

Merchants
- Merchant families: family (tap), home town, caravans owned, wagons on the road, cargo value out, purse, profit this season, robbed this season.
- Arms trade: contracts open, seller -> buyer faction, goods, delivered / due.

Census
- World population each season, with children / adults / elders, female / male, births, deaths by cause, by race and by faction.
- Same "Births and deaths" and "Coming and going" cards as the town Census (town spec part 2), summed for the world or the selected faction, over time. Table of towns (sortable by any column): people, births, deaths per cause per 1,000, left, arrived, net. Sorting by "starved" or "battle" shows at once where and why people are dying. Moves between factions shown as flows (from -> to, count, why).

3. FACTION VIEW (selector on a faction)
Same tabs filtered to that faction's towns and forces, plus Army: rank tree with the culture's rank names and counts per rank, wage bill per rank, promotions and successions this season (with the free-for-all fights for orcs), armies and their commanders (tap -> person).

4. TESTS (headless)
- Every death has exactly one cause; world deaths by cause = sum of towns; births - deaths + arrived - left = population change for each town, faction and the world.
- World totals = sum of town totals (people, goods, acres, treasury) on the same day.
- Every force on the map appears exactly once in On the move; every caravan has a merchant family owner.
- Selector lists every town once, under its current faction (a captured town moves).
