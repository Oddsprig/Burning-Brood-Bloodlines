BURNING BROOD: BLOODLINES v0.016
Updated: 2026-10-11 | combined combat history and officer command build

START
Use the Desktop shortcut Burning Brood Bloodlines v0.016 (green dragon/crown).
It opens the versioned Burning_Brood_Bloodlines_v0.016 folder on your Desktop.
To install another extracted copy, run Install_Desktop.cmd. Extract the whole
Windows ZIP, then double-click Launch_Bloodlines.cmd; keep game/ and runtime/
beside it. First launch prepares quietly. Windows64-bit Godot4.7.2 is bundled.
Saves use the separate Burning Brood Bloodlines user-data folder.

THIS VERSION - v015 + v016 TOGETHER
Combat log opens during a battle, holding playback while you read. Back to battle
or Esc restores the previous pause state. Open the unit inspector's Full combat
history button for that person directly. At the end, expand Combat log beside
the other result details. Find a name, choose the actual combatant, and use
First/Previous/Next/Last.18 chronological outcomes are built per page; Details
opens actual tissue damage, armour/weapon consequences and calculation records.
Names remain linked to the same participant even after death or duplicate names.
Reading never repeats an attack, changes the battle tick or consumes random rolls.

The complete record is captured before the old8192-event/512-person tails trim.
Old tails remain only for live presentation. The current fight uses private
transient chunks and participant indexes; resident journal records are bounded
at1024. This is a record bound, not a total RAM/byte limit. No unlimited permanent
battle archive is created. Continue, world replacement and battle exit release
history/files; an IO failure is visibly labelled incomplete rather than hidden.
The right performance panel includes log capture/page cost and retained counters.

On BOTH sides, a standing, fighting officer supports actual soldiers assigned to
that command within the native command radius. Scheduled morale checks call
fleeing men back to the fight, reset their fleeing orders and count their return
once. Native wounds, blood, death and unconsciousness remain real: rally neither
heals nor resurrects, and cannot return troops who already left the battlefield.
Officer death, incapacity or flight removes that support. Campaign field-army,
militia and guard rank trees now supply their actual Unit.squad links; company
and named bandit commands retain their own identities. No new men/ranks/funds
are created by linking or rallying. Autonomous morale recovery remains available.

The v014 compact both-side casualty/loot summary remains. All five detail headings
are closed initially; only one section/page is built at a time. Continue remains
reachable. Automatic event slow motion remains OFF by default; opt in via
Esc/Settings -> Gameplay. Your chosen playback speed and pause remain intact.
v011 campaign responsiveness and v012 consistent sliced saves/ownership remain.
Current actual frame/world/cleanup results and practical limits are recorded in
test-results/v0.016_2026-10-11_results.txt; package/Desktop receipts are separate.
No artwork, old-save migration or phone export work belongs to this release.

Dragons receive daily feeding, shelter supplies, condition and continuous growth.
Their actual owning household buys meat and wood from the location market; a
living custodian must be present. Human food reserves are protected. House ->
Dragons & eggs shows the current quote and last actual payment separately.
Advance campaign time to process care. Shortages lower health and stall growth;
prolonged neglect can kill. Supplied dragons recover. Growth changes physical
mass, strength, bite force and tooth/claw dimensions while gem hardness stays
fixed. Full care takes 20 native calendar years per size band, up to Colossal.
Eggs need shelter care, consume no meat and do not hatch in this pass.
Gifted/inherited holdings charge the new owner's actual household. Saves retain
care, growth and daily payment guards. A missing keeper can be appointed through
Choose custodian. Buying care does not grant a rider bond.

Copper Oath v0.053 humanoid art now supplies units and portraits: humans,
high/moon/dark elves, dwarves, orcs, goblins and trolls. Includes both women's
drawing sets, male beard styles, fitted clothing/armour, rebuilt open helmets
and the enclosed closed helmet. Real metal and forge-maker identity determine

Bound ruler portraits use the same real unit appearance and worn equipment.
Household-only rulers retain stable portraits without creating combat units.

House -> Dragons & eggs opens your family's individual holdings and the realm
roster. Dragons have two hind legs and wings as forelimbs, with varied original
builds and sizes. Egg colours use gem names: Ruby Egg, Sapphire Egg, Black Diamond
Egg, Emerald Egg, Amethyst Egg and more. Inspect parents, age, owner, keeper and
location. A gift changes ownership; it leaves the keeper and location as recorded.
Living dragons have personal names separate from types such as Sapphire Dragon.
Type sets tooth/claw hardness; size, strength, bite force and tooth/claw dimensions
are calculated separately in the details. Native tactical dragon bodies follow.
Dragon ownership follows legitimate family succession; it grants no rider bond.
Model preview now uses six coherent organic sprites with original proportions.
Horns match the gem type: Sapphire horns are sapphire blue. Teeth and claws
also use gem colours; scales and skin remain organic.
Play bite/recoil movement, view broad blood and mangled limbs, head separation
at the neck, tiny tooth/claw chips and existing-unit saddle fit. Wings/limbs stay
attached when mangled. Only head separation removes head/jaw. Riders hold saddle
handles; no rope to the head. Full leg gait and actual riding/battles follow.
16 compact generated type portraits supplement the native head portraits.
The earlier v0.004 draft humanoid art has been replaced by released v0.053 art.
Rider bonds and dragon battles follow in separate roadmap versions.

FIRST CAMPAIGN
New Campaign -> Normal / 1 day for a quick start -> choose an existing city
and its real ruling family. Larger worlds and longer histories take longer.
The map keeps your current ruler portrait near the lower left.
House -> Realm politics shows your lord, liege, sovereign, heir and loyalty.
Taxes & defence shows actual private/public budgets and current tax estimates.
Set 2%, 5% or 10% estate tax (one decree per week), fund your city treasury,
and order existing militia to muster, stand down or use automatic defence.
Only the sovereign can fund the realm treasury from their own house purse.
These are ordinary public funds; your travelling company's wallet stays separate.
Stand-down returns militia to civilian work; professional guards keep their duty.
Monthly estimates do not create income. The actual levy protects essential food.
Population shows people, common-folk savings/jobs and the town's tracked families.
Businesses belong to tracked families; common folk provide most of the labour.
Public service operators draw wages from the actual city/realm funding account.
Monthly common-folk wealth tax goes to the actual ruling family. Tribute goes up
the hierarchy. House, town, faction and army accounts remain separate owners.
You can fund your army from your ruling household, and take paid political actions.
Humans/dwarves: sons' lines first, daughters as fallback. Elves: eldest child's
line. Orcs retain native strength contests. Defection changes real allegiance.
The company starts with 100 real people. Supported field-force targets are
100 / 150 / 200 by world size, with individual material/body/wound battles.
Bronze-capable smiths stop making copper/tin gear; market gear becomes real scrap
and alloy inputs. Existing issued/legendary gear retains its history.
Creature encounters, movement and battles are temporarily suspended.
