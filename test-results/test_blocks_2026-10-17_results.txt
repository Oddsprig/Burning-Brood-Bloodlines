WHAT CHANGED (user rule 2026-10-17: tests that can share one run do, and a whole block takes under 60 s, 180 s hard cap)
  tests/runner.gd runs several tests in ONE Godot process: -- --block=<name> (tests/blocks.json) or -- --tests=a.gd,b.gd.
  Between tests it frees the last test's scenes and resets the shared state tests change (reset_between). Each test keeps
  its own 180 s watchdog and its own script-error count; the block ends with a summary line.
  Cut-down tests (same checks, smaller setups):
    claude_rounds.gd       DevTools.set_round_size(10): 10-a-side rounds instead of the game's 100, so about 15 a side
                           proves the rounds (was 110 v 110: 91 s; now 17 s in its block)
    claude_campaign.gd     army of 12 instead of 40 (41 s -> 36 s, including the block's first world load)
    claude_dragonfear.gd   army of 12 instead of 40 (28 s -> 13 s)
    claude_prisoners_ui_v033.gd  the real battle fights 10 of the enemy band (38 s -> 18 s)
  BattleRounds.round_size (static, default ROUND = 100) is what the game uses for a round; only DevTools changes it.
