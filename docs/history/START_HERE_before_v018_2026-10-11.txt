BURNING BROOD: BLOODLINES v0.017
Updated: 2026-10-11 | funded army raising and real home returns

START
Use Burning Brood Bloodlines v0.017 on your actual Desktop (green dragon/crown).
It opens Desktop/Burning_Brood_Bloodlines_v0.017. For another extracted copy,
run Install_Desktop.cmd, or Launch_Bloodlines.cmd with game/ and runtime/ beside
it. Official Windows64 Godot4.7.2 is bundled; first launch prepares quietly.
Use a fresh Normal world with1 history day. Choose its existing city/ruling house.
Saves use the separate Bloodlines user-data folder; tests use private paths.

THIS VERSION
A new campaign and house selection leave your private field army UNRAISED.
There are no free100 people,75000 army coins, invented equipment or provisions.
Public city guards and militia are separate real forces. Your ruler portrait
remains near the map's lower left; Army opens the new army management screen.

At your actual home settlement, fund the army from your house's real private
purse. Review how many ADDITIONAL people can depart, actual available equipment,
food/signing costs and three days of total field wages. Confirm the review to
raise those real people. A quote/cancel spends nothing. Capacity is200 TOTAL,
with up to8 real officers. A shortage gives an honest partial muster and receipt.
No automatic transfer from house, city or realm treasuries occurs.

Return to the actual home settlement and stand down. The same people, bodies,
wounds, family identities and private equipment stay recorded at home. Borrowed
city kit returns to its actual armory; living residents rejoin civilian work
once and stop drawing field wages. Re-raising retains their physical identity.
Dismissal follows the same home return. Native aging, sickness and death still
apply. Private equipment of natural deaths remains in saved household-linked
estate custody; management of those estates is a later action, not a market sale.
Remote records cannot move your company home or authorize a remote return.

RETAINED GAMEPLAY
House/army/city/realm funds have separate owners. Businesses/land/herds belong
to tracked families; common folk provide most work and their pooled wealth pays
real local taxes and tribute. Native material/tissue/body/wound/blood battles,
100-200-person forces, feudal allegiance and defection remain authoritative.
Automatic battle slow motion defaults OFF (Esc/Settings -> Gameplay to enable).
Paused/end Combat log records the full fight, with18-row pages, character filters
and native damage details. Results show compact casualties and expandable detail.
Standing fighting officers rally assigned fleeing followers on BOTH sides;
death/incapacity/withdrawal removes support. Reading logs never reruns combat.
The right performance panel shows frame/FPS, calculation times, jobs and memory.
Army interface cost and Army management stage are now included.

Copper Oath v0.053 released humanoid races, genders, hair/beards, weapons and
armour remain exact. Dragons/eggs keep individual gem types, owners, care and
growth; organic sprites have gem-coloured horns/teeth/claws. Head/jaw movement,
blood/mangling and saddle handles remain; full gait/riders/battles are later.
Background monsters remain suspended. Bronze smiths recycle counted inputs.
