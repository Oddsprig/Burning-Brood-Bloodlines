About the game

Your house is part of a wider world. Choose a settlement and an existing local household, then manage the people, private money and obligations that come with it. A ruling house and an ordinary household have different rights. Choosing a house does not hand you a free field army.

Follow the money from household purses to army pay, town markets and local rulers. Soldiers come from the world's people; food, equipment and wages have to come from somewhere. A small house can borrow to get started, then fund and raise its own soldiers.

Dragons are individuals with owners, locations and rider bonds. Their care draws on real supplies. Bond a dragon with a living local person and bring it into the same automatic battles as your soldiers, with its rider in the saddle at the base of the neck.

Prepare your force and watch it fight. Weapons, armour, materials and body injuries affect combat; morale and officers affect who holds and who breaks.

  • A real household. Choose a local family and customize its shown name, banner and ruler.

  • Family and law. Court, marriage and succession screens for your house.

Putting it on your Android phone

Nepo Dragons is not on the Play Store, so your phone asks once before installing it.

  1. Download the APK on your phone and open it from the download notice or from Files, then Downloads.
  2. If asked, tap Settings, turn on "Allow from this source", then go back.
  3. Tap Install. If Play Protect warns about an unknown app, tap "More details", then "Install anyway".

To update later, install the new APK over the old one. Your campaign stays.
