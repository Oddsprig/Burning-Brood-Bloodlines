WHAT CHANGED (by system)
Battle presentation (battle/battle.gd, map_view.gd, unit_view.gd, breath_fx.gd)
- Follow camera frames every living, unrouted fighter of both sides and eases
  in; dragging, wheel or keys take control, C hands it back. Battles open
  already framed.
- Ground is painted once into a texture (SubViewport, UPDATE_ONCE) instead of
  thousands of canvas commands every frame; checkerboard replaced by soft
  patches. Sieges draw town walls and towers beyond the defenders' edge;
  raids draw houses.
- All ground shadows in one canvas item; settled corpses skip per-frame doll
  work. Battle feed: 4 compact lines (full text stays in the Combat log).
- Developer performance overlay hidden by default (F3 / Settings).
Dragons in battle (sim/dragon_breath.gd, battle/dragon_view.gd)
- Breath: each gem line breathes one element (fire, frost, venom, storm).
  A 6-tile cone, 2-5 charges per battle by growth, 4.5 s cooldown, visible
  wind-up. Damage goes into the same BodyState layers as weapons (skin, fat,
  muscle; pain, bleeding, knock-out, death checks). Armour matters per
  element (metal resists fire, conducts cold and lightning; venom eats gear).
  Allies caught in the cone are burned (reduced strength); the dragon will
  not breathe when more friends than foes are in front of it. Dragon fear
  shocks enemy morale. Every breath and burn is in the combat journal.
- The bonded rider is drawn seated on the saddle (MountedRiderArt layers at
  human scale; far leg behind the dragon, saddle and near side in front),
  following the seat through every pose.
Campaign warfare (campaign/warfare.gd, world_map.gd)
- Tap a foreign settlement with your raised army nearby: Raid or Besiege.
  Defenders are the town's real guards and militia (capped at 1.25x your
  army, 12..200). Siege defenders hold the walls.
- Raid win: 35% of the treasury and 12% of household purses go to the army
  purse (coin moves, never created), 40% of stored grain burns, ~3% of the
  common folk die (Population.kill_slots, recorded in the census), unrest
  rises, the town's loyalty to its own lord falls and grievance rises.
- Siege win: the settlement swears to your realm (forces split by home town,
  feudal hierarchy rebuilt, diplomatic grievance, academy sync); it starts at
  loyalty 30 / grievance 45 and can defect again through the native daily
  loyalty rules. A realm with no towns left is broken.
- Either act sours relations (Diplomacy.on_fight, on_town_attacked).
- Results screen leads with the assault outcome.
Dragons on campaign (warfare.gd march, dragon_combat.gd battle_units)
- "March with army": a bonded adult joins every battle the field army fights
  (not only home defence). It eats from the army purse every day
  (6 x linear scale^2 coins); unfed dragons lose care health. Recalled when
  the army stands down or by the button.
Dragon lifecycle (campaign/dragon_life.gd, dragon_care.gd, dragon_growth.gd)
- Eggs incubate with good care and hatch after 40 days into a named small
  hatchling with a combat body; juveniles become adults after one 120-day
  year; growth is one size band per year when well fed (was 20 years, which
  no campaign could reach). A healthy female + male of one house at one roost
  may lay a clutch (one per 150 days, about one per season while eligible).
  Runs only on days DragonCare actually settles.
Ambitions and outcome (campaign/ambitions.gd, ui/ambitions_view.gd, victory)
- Eight ambitions measured from real state; six = "Dynasty Triumphant"
  victory screen (keep playing allowed). Extinction of the founding dynasty
  remains the defeat. Toasts on the map when one is achieved.
Onboarding (ui/guide_view.gd, title "How to play", map "Help")
- 7-page guide opens once on the first map visit.
Performance (art/dragon_art.gd, dragon_rig_art.gd, ui/dragon_card.gd)
- Dragon pigment (a per-pixel pass over large source art) is cached on disk
  (user://dragon_tints) and pre-painted on worker threads at new game/load.
  Part extraction uses image blits with cached masks. Cards compute 2 pose
  models instead of 8. Dragon card: about 1.5 s -> under 0.3 s.
Marriage, family and alliances (campaign/marriage.gd, ui/court.gd) - pass 2
- Court screen: ruler, spouse, heir, all living members with marriage status and
  children; Find a match / Arrange betrothal; alliances, claims, chronicle.
- Candidates: unmarried people of other ruling houses and tracked landed
  families (not their heads or heirs). Deterministic acceptance with visible
  reasons; gift tiers (2% / 6% of the house purse, real coin to their house).
- Wedding moves the spouse's native person record into your household (new
  local pid when changing town; identity, birth house and kin UIDs kept), so
  the native childbearing rules produce children of your house and heirs under
  your law. Dowry (3% of their purse) is real coin. A dragon house may send an
  egg. Betrothals wed automatically at coming of age.
- Alliance: +25 relations / +15 standing with a foreign realm; vassal towns
  +15 loyalty, -10 grievance and +0.15 loyalty a day. Claims on the spouse's
  ruling seat make a siege conquest welcome (loyalty 60 instead of 30).
Large-world performance (pass 2, measured on the player's real 135 MB save)
- Town reserves capped at 6 + 5 per size step; surplus reservists return home
  (same people); the armory keeps kit for its reserve plus a levy of 30 + 40 per
  size step; surplus armory pieces are melted into scrap metal stock by mass
  (legendary pieces always kept). Dragon roster paged (9 per page) with cached
  card models.
Audio (core/sound.gd, audio/CREDITS.txt) - pass 2

Look (ui/theme.gd, ui/title.gd, battle) - pass 2

KNOWN LIMITS (honest)
- Dense battles (400 people + 2 dragons, 2560x1440) average about 55 FPS:
  median frames hit the 60 cap, but a simulation tick (about 5-6 ms of
  GDScript for 400 fighters) lands every third frame and some frames reach
  25-30 ms. Not a locked 60.
- Map: midnight work is sliced, but rare militia/creature checks still cost
  up to ~150 ms (occasional hitch at x4 speed).
- The rider is visual and flavour on the dragon; the rider is not a separate
  target in the battle sim and does not die when the dragon dies.
- The first view of each dragon variety still paints its pigment once (on a
  worker thread at game start/load; cached on disk afterwards).
- Ruling lords of conquered towns keep their seats as your vassals; there is
  no title transfer to your own family yet.
- Marriage is player-driven for your house; other houses still marry by the
  native in-town rules. No divorce, concubines or AI cross-realm matchmaking.
- Older test files test_battle_screen.gd and test_battle_presentation_state.gd
  already failed on the untouched v0.022 baseline (verified) and still do.

ROUND-BASED BATTLES AND INTERFACE PASS 2 (2026-10-16)
- campaign/battle_rounds.gd: a battle with more than 100 on a side runs as
  rounds. State lives in Game.pending_battle["rounds"]; the battle scene
  reloads for each round with pending_battle["round_side0"] and ["enemy"]
  set to that round's unit of up to 100 fit men (fresh men first). After
  each round: dead stay dead, men downed on a field the other side holds
  become prisoners (world["captives"] for yours, world["prisoners"] for the
  enemy's), wounded who cannot fight sit out, morale moves with the round
  result, men lost, officers lost and the commander. Break at morale < 25,
  no fit men, or > 60% of starting men dead or taken.
  Game.finish_battle gets one merged result (BattleRounds.final_result).
- Retreat: World._battle_result now sends a beaten people band to its home
  town (BattleRounds.retreat_band: rest and regroup for 5 days) instead of
  deleting it. A beaten player army returns every survivor home as
  reserves (Mobilization.return_home) and the party stands at the seat.
- Off-screen army clashes use campaign/clash_rounds.gd (written by Sol 6.1
  through the bridge, reviewed and formatted here) with the same rules;
  the loser regroups at home instead of being deleted.
- Mobilization.enlist_household: an adult of your house joins the army as
  an officer; the person is the unit, so wounds, capture and death are
  theirs. Captives show "held captive" in the Court tree.
- ui/round_summary.gd (written by Muse Wonder Full through the bridge):
  the round card and the separate battle card. The Muster "Your house"
  card is also Muse's. Laws screen layout is Sol's; the army tiles Muse's.
- Caps: Mobilization.LIMIT 1000; AI field armies FIELD_LIMIT 600, growing
  50 a week. The battle field never holds more than 100 a side.
- Known gaps: prisoners are recorded but there is no ransom or exchange
  screen yet; captured enemy nobles are not yet offered for ransom.
