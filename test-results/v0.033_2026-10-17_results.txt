NEW TESTS
tests/claude_prisoners_v033.gd - real generated world (seed 4242), the most populous ruling seat:
  capture through BattleRounds.take_one_clash (win, loss, draw, raid/siege defenders not taken);
  weekly Persons.sweep / Army.sweep and the family sync keep held people alive and counted;
  eight days pass; ransom (coin from their realm to your house purse once, standing, the man
  home with his family, off his realm's roll); a realm without coin is refused with no change;
  a noble is not traded for a soldier; a changed review is rejected; save/load keeps both lists
  and the held body (blood level); noble-for-noble exchange (both home, no coin or standing);
  paying a ransom (short purse refused; coin to the captor once; home as a reserve, counted
  at home); release (standing once per realm per day); death in captivity drops and logs the
  record; an enemy prisoner whose register row moved stays held; a pre-v0.033 record cannot be
  bargained for.
  Mutation check: with the Persons.live_uids fix removed, 6 checks fail (captive dead after the
  sweep, register row lost, family count, prisoners lost within a week).
tests/claude_prisoners_ui_v033.gd - through main.tscn: Court lists the realm, Bargain opens the
  window, the Ransom and Pay ransom buttons complete real bargains, the Court notice reports it;
  then a real one-clash battle through the battle scene takes exactly the loser's downed men and
  the results screen says "Taken prisoner".
tests/claude_rounds.gd - now also checks that every prisoner and captive record from a real
  round battle keeps its actual Unit.
