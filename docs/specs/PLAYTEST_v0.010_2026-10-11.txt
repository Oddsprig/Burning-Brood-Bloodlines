SCRAP / WHICH ECONOMY PAYS?
User asked where sold scrap goes and whose economy buys it; no replacement
sale policy was requested. Read-only source inspection answered:
game/core/game.gd:finish_battle estimates loot, keeps selected gear in stash,
collects material mass and passes the result to CampaignWorld.on_battle_result.
game/campaign/town_econ.gd:add_scrap chooses nearest economy-enabled town
without a faction filter and adds counted kilograms to its market stock.
game/campaign/world.gd:_battle_result reprices scrap at local sell prices and
caps the sales payout by Goods.market_gold; Goods.pay_market debits that market
business's till and company gold receives the actual payout. Kept gear stays
in stash; coin on bodies is accounted separately. Sale is immediate, no transport
or buyer selection. The screenshot alone does not identify the actual town.
Image:codex-clipboard-97724d9d-c2df-412d-8af9-d217e8e46b6e.png.
Queued:v020 visible sale/ownership receipts and conservation review. Proposed
future carry-to-market salvage is creative planning, not a quoted user requirement.
