Burning Brood: Bloodlines - Claude Edition
Player's Guide

STARTING
Launch the game and choose New Campaign. Set the world options, then
pick a settlement on the map. Pick one of its houses, choose Customize
for your house name, emblem and banner colours, check everything on
Review, then choose Begin. A short guide opens on your first map visit
(Help on the map shows it again).

THE MAP
Tap a destination to travel there. Use Wait to let time pass where you
are, and speed x1/x2/x4 to control how fast days run. The map buttons
are Find, House, Army, Dragons, Ambitions, Help, Records, Save and
Menu. Tap any settlement to see its lord, liege, loyalty and garrison
before you decide what to do there.

RAISING AN ARMY
Raise an army only at home. Open Army, fund the army from the house
purse, then review the muster before you confirm. The muster lists real
residents by name, with their kit, food and 3 days of wages. Confirm to
take them into service. Stand down at home to return the same people to
their work. The army purse pays daily wages while they serve, so keep
it funded. Soldiers who die are gone for good and lower the population
of their home settlement. An army can grow to 1000.
Under "Your house" on the Army screen, adult family members can ride
with the army as officers. Your ruler, if riding, commands it. They fight
in the rounds like anyone else: they gain experience and can be wounded,
taken prisoner or killed (which changes your succession).

DRAGONS
Open the Dragons screen to manage your brood. Bond an adult dragon to a
rider who is present at its roost. Set a dragon to Defend this
settlement to guard its home, or to March with army to travel and fight
with your force. A marching dragon eats from the army purse daily, so
budget for its upkeep. Eggs hatch after 40 days of good care.
Hatchlings become adults after one 120-day year. A healthy female and
male pair kept at one roost may lay a clutch of new eggs.

COURT AND MARRIAGE
Open Court (map) or "Court & marriages" (House). It shows your ruler, spouse,
heir and every living member of your house. For an unmarried adult choose
Find a match; for a child, Arrange betrothal. Candidates come from the ruling
houses and landed families of every settlement, and each shows whether they
would accept and why (rank, realm, standing, your dragons, age gap). Add a
modest or generous gift from your house purse to sweeten an offer. A match
brings the spouse into your household with a dowry (and sometimes a dragon
egg); their children are born into your house and become your heirs. The
spouse's house becomes an ally: rival realms warm to you, vassals keep faith,
and if they rule a settlement your house gains a claim there - besiege it
and the town accepts you as rightful. A refusal costs a little standing.

PRISONERS AND RANSOM
Men left downed on a field the other side holds are taken prisoner, in
battles of every size (not a town's guards in a raid or siege). The battle
results say how many. At Court, Prisoners lists each realm: press Bargain.
For their people you hold: Ransom (their house or realm pays your house
purse) or Release (free). Either raises your standing with that realm,
once a day. For your people
they hold: Pay ransom from your house purse, or Exchange one of theirs for
one of yours - they will not hand back a noble for a common soldier.
Prices: soldier 6, officer 20, noble 60, ruler 150 coins. Freed people of
yours come home as reserves with their own wounds and kit.

BATTLE
Battles are automatic: your fighters act on their own and you watch and
inspect. Use pause, 1x, 2x, 4x or Skip to control the pace, and read
the Combat log to follow what happened. Tap a fighter to inspect them.
The camera follows the fight on its own. Drag or use the mouse wheel to
take over the camera, and press C to resume following. Dragons bite and
claw, and breathe their gem element: fire, frost, venom or lightning.
Dragon breath does not spare friends, so it can burn your own troops
if they are caught in it.

ROUNDS
A battle with more than 100 on either side is fought in rounds. Each
round, one unit of up to 100 from each army meets the other and they
fight until that clash is finished. Then the next pair goes into the
fray, fresh men first. Survivors fall back to their army. The wounded
sit out every later round. Men knocked down on a field the enemy holds
are taken prisoner. After each round a round summary shows the losses,
both armies' morale, who is still waiting to fight and what happened to
your family. Losing a round, losing men and losing officers cost morale,
and the commander's fall costs most. An army breaks when its morale
drops too low, it has nobody left to send, or it has lost more than 60%
of its men. A separate battle summary follows the last round.
The beaten army does not die where it stands. Everyone not killed or
captured goes straight back to the seat of whoever owns the army and
must remuster there. Your own beaten men return home as reserves; raise
them again on the Army screen. A beaten enemy band stands at its home
town, regrouping for 5 days before it can march. Armies that meet each
other off-screen fight by the same round rules.

WAR
To make war, march your raised army to a foreign settlement and tap it.
Choose Raid to take coin, burn grain and kill residents, then withdraw.
Choose Besiege to attack defenders holding the walls: a win brings the
town into your realm. Both Raid and Besiege anger the settlement's
realm, which may answer in kind. Conquered towns start resentful, and a
resentful town may defect from your realm later if loyalty stays low.

AMBITIONS AND VICTORY
Your house pursues 8 ambitions, listed on the Ambitions screen. Achieve
6 of them to earn Dynasty Triumphant. Victory does not end the game:
you can keep playing for as long as you wish. You lose only when your
founding dynasty has no living members left, so protect your family
and keep heirs alive (House -> Laws shows your heir).

SAVING
Choose Save on the map to save at any time. The game also autosaves
when you arrive at a town. To return to a saved game, choose Continue
on the title screen.

KEYS
WASD or the arrow keys pan the map. The mouse wheel zooms in and out.
Esc opens settings (music and sound volume, gore, slow motion). F3 toggles
the performance overlay. In battle, C
resumes camera following after you have dragged or zoomed the view.

Good luck, and may your bloodline endure.
