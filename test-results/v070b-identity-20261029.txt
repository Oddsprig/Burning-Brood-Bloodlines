Mismatches fixed
1. Prisoner/captive physical holders were absent from Dynasty.bound_unit: their
   court/map portraits fell back to civilian seeds and default/empty equipment.
   Lookup now uses both held-unit collections with UID/home/family/race guards.
2. Dragon rider layers were cached by person UID alone. Compare the full saved
   display spec too, rebuilding for changed face, equipment, age or wounds.
3. Portrait.refresh erased set_spec portraits, and set_spec retained the caller's
   mutable dictionary. Preserve the portrait snapshot and copy incoming specs.
4. Paperdoll.set_spec overwrote the caller's team field. Copy before adapting it.
5. PartyIcon recoloured a real leader's clothing using an extra vivid-colour pass.
6. Civilian designer variants were derived before the final seed override.
7. Custom house colours differed between civilian court and campaign portraits,
   and could replace a bound unit's actual clothing palette on the campaign card.
   Civilian readers share the saved palette; bound readers retain the native spec.
8. Field/captive units' cached ages could disagree with registered-person portraits.
   Unit display inputs now derive age from the saved birthday/current campaign day.
