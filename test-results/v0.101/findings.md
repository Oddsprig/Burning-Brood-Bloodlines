# v0.101 review findings (Grok 4.7 high + Sol 6.1 max, both read-only, 2026-11-07)
Reviewed: game-code diff of v0.098 + v0.099 (review_v098_v099.diff). Each item below was confirmed by both reviewers unless noted.

F1 (high) campaign/marriage.gd arrange_ai (~530), try_ai_match (~555): AI gate rejects age gaps > 16 years; score (~496-498) penalizes gaps > 8. Neither uses age_gap_scale (the player list at ~170/~205 does). Elf/dwarf AI houses rarely wed. Fix: use 16.0 * age_gap_scale(race_a, race_b) in both gates and raw_gap / scale > 8 in the score.
F2 (high) campaign/marriage.gd ~146-147 and ~167-168: betrothal window is a fixed human 6 years before adulthood (+4 for candidates); ~114 advertises betrothal 8 years before adulthood, so an elf of 32-33 sees the action with an empty list, and an elf of 30 / dwarf of 18 gets nothing. Fix: one race-aware betrothal-eligibility helper used by all three checks (scale 6, +4 and 8 by age_gap_scale(race, race)).
F3 (high, Sol's narrowed version) campaign/dragon_combat.gd ~302-306 seats a bonded rider even when the rider's unit was not picked for this round; battle/battle.gd ~1140 drops seat_joined fighters from fighter_units, and campaign/battle_rounds.gd ~140-169 derives casualties, commander loss and household accounting only from that array. A reserve commander killed while mounted adds no death or officer-loss morale. NOT a bug (intended): fit dragons may re-enter every round, and fled units may fight again (battle_rounds ~83, ~161). Persistent wounds/deaths ARE settled via dragon_combat ~537-593. Fix: count seated pool members in round accounting once per unit (or seat a rider only when his unit is in this round's picks: pick the simpler, safer one and say which).
F4 (high, static) phone landscape (844x390 and 1280x720 must both work):
  a. ui/dynasty_card.gd:43 map card min 430x186; campaign/world_map.gd ~444 nav buttons 96px wrap to 2 rows ~522-535; card placed at ~626-630 goes above the top of the screen. Fix: cap the card height to the space between top bar and nav (or one scrolling nav row on short screens).
  b. battle/battle.gd ~874 toast min 660x96, up to 3 kept (~38, ~893): taller than a 390px screen and eats taps. Fix: derive toast width and count from the safe area.
  c. ui/town_market_tab.gd ~207-220, ~288: stall columns x1.5 = ~930px min; its scroll (~101) has horizontal scrolling off via ui/kit.gd ~104. Fix: enable horizontal scrolling for this stock view.
F5 (Sol only, high, pre-existing but widened by v0.099) campaign/marriage.gd ~158-170 lets the player marry across races; the wedding moves the spouse into the house's family (~275, ~307-308) and dynasty.gd ~146 then reports the family's race, so an elf spouse ages and dies by human rules (persons.gd ~650-690). Preferred fix: keep each married-in person's own race (if a per-person race field exists or can be added safely without breaking saves); otherwise reject mixed-race candidates for the player as the AI already does (~529). Say which you did and why.
