LOCKED USER REQUIREMENTS
CK3/AGOT-style feudal dynasty game with armies, economy, dragons and actual riders.
Dragons have wyvern anatomy: two hind legs and two wings as forelimbs, no extra
front legs. Use original varied builds, sizes and colours. Eggs are named after
gem colours: Ruby Egg, Sapphire Egg, Black Diamond Egg, Emerald Egg and more.
Living types use gem names too: Sapphire Dragon, Ruby Dragon, Black Diamond
Dragon. Personal dragon names are separate. Horns match their gem type: Sapphire
horns are sapphire blue. Teeth/claws also use gem colours; skin/scales stay organic.
Type sets tooth/claw hardness;
body mass/size, muscle strength, bite force, tooth/claw dimensions, contact
area and penetration determine the strike independently. Hardness is separate
from fracture resistance; steel armour still faces the complete native strike.
Every town belongs to its faction's lord/liege/king, warlord or emperor hierarchy.
Intrigue, loyalty and defection affect actual offices, land and faction allegiance.
Human/dwarf male-preference primogeniture allows daughters when sons' lines fail;
elves inherit eldest-child first; orcs keep their existing strength rules.
Present succession in Laws, not in the starting house chooser.
Common folk are the majority, in race-based population/work/wealth pools. Keep
10-20 tracked families/dynasties per town; tracked people own town businesses,
land and herds, and common folk supply most labour. Track aggregate private coin,
food, jobs and taxes paid to the actual baron/lord family, then tribute upward.
Armies target 100-200 per side. Recruit from real population; charge real pay,
food and equipment; losses reduce original population once. Tactical battles use
native material/tissue/body-part/wound/blood calculations, also for dragons.
Starting house selection must not automatically raise a player field army.
Bronze unlock replaces new copper/tin gear production and recycles counted inputs;
businesses/shops remain. Background creature movement/encounters stay suspended.
Choose a settlement and see its map location, then choose a house within it;
offer Customize House. Keep the ruler portrait near map lower left.
Preserve both phone orientations. Phone export is roughly 50 passes away, not a
routine release gate. Import current Copper Oath models now; redesign them later.
