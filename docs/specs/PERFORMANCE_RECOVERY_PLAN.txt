COMBAT LOG CONTRACT
Open Battle log while paused; Read battle log remains on the finished battle.
Opening for reading holds simulation; closing does not silently resume it.
Chronological narrative, filters (unit/side/outcome) and expandable technical
details explain actual weapon/material, struck body part, armour reaction, tissue
wound, blood/function loss, broken kit, teeth/limbs lost, deaths and retreats.
Use recorded results through deterministic CombatText; no invented strikes,
extra RNG, fresh damage calculations or live history rewritten by later wounds.
Existing army caps:8192 battle events and512 entries/fighter. Keep storage and
visible page size bounded. Latest user requires the whole fight and individual
character filtering. Capture before caps discard events; use chunked per-battle
storage so bounded memory does not mean incomplete history. Preserve exact
event order/identity; never call a truncated log complete. Unlimited permanent
battle archives remain outside scope; record retrieval limits honestly.
Do not build hundreds of hidden UI rows or reformat the entire log every frame.
game/battle/battle.gd:675 build_result removes lodged arrows and applies growth;
reading/filtering must never call it. Tick/RNG/bodies/items stay unchanged.
