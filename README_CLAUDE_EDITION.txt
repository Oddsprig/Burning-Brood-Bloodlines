BURNING BROOD: BLOODLINES - CLAUDE EDITION
A dynasty, war and dragon game: lead a real house in a living feudal world.

PLAY
Double-click "Burning Brood Bloodlines - Claude Edition" on the Desktop, or
run Launch_Claude_Edition.cmd in the Burning_Brood_Bloodlines_Claude_Edition_
Windows folder. The first launch prepares for about 10 seconds. Choose New
Campaign (Normal world, 1 history day is quickest), pick a settlement and a
house, customize and Begin. A guide opens on your first map visit; Help on
the map and "How to play" on the title screen show it again.
Full player guide: HOW_TO_PLAY.txt.

WHAT IS NEW COMPARED WITH v0.022
- Dragons fight for real: gem breath (fire, frost, venom, lightning) that
  wounds through the body system and can burn your own troops; the bonded
  rider sits on the saddle in battle; dragons can march with your army
  anywhere and eat from its purse.
- A full dragon lifecycle: eggs hatch, hatchlings grow up in a campaign year,
  paired adults lay new clutches.
- War that changes the world: raid a settlement for coin and grain (its
  people and lord suffer), or besiege it and bring it into your realm;
  conquered towns resent you and may defect.
- Goals: eight ambitions, six for victory (Dynasty Triumphant).
- Battle camera that follows the fight; a cleaner battlefield with walls for
  sieges and houses for raids; compact battle feed; debug overlay hidden (F3).
- Faster: dragon screens open 5x faster; far fewer draw objects in battle.
