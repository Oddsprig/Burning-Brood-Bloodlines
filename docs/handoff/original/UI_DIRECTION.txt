BURNING BROOD: BLOODLINES - UI DIRECTION
Concept only. Apply to the separate game's copy during implementation.
UI_CONCEPT.svg illustrates the hierarchy and palette with fictional data.

VISUAL TARGET
A dark court/dragon-house atmosphere: deep charcoal, forest/emerald greens,
royal purples, pale readable text, restrained gold and warm danger accents.
Green expresses city/estate/supplies; purple expresses house/court/dragons.
Use labelled states as well as colour so players can read their meaning.

Use pale text on action backgrounds. Avoid dark text on dark panels.
Keep large numeric values aligned, use separators, and give units once in a
column heading. Show concise labels plus detail-on-tap; explanatory paragraphs
belong in expandable details. Risk must use a word/icon, never colour alone.

INFORMATION HIERARCHY
Persistent summary: date, ruling house, city, city treasury, personal funds and
urgent problems. Keep private/public accounts visibly separate.
Desktop primary destinations: City, House, Dragons, Court, Relations, Armies,
Records. Phone: a small persistent navigation bar and a More destination;
avoid squeezing seven tabs onto one narrow row.
City dashboard: a few useful metric cards, supply/treasury bars and local issues.
House: real portraits, ruler/heir/spouse links, family tree, office/claim badges.
Dragons: cards with age/stage, actual rider, owner, health, food demand and
availability. Filter by stage/status. One selected dragon has a clear detail
page with parentage, care, bond and war assignment.
Court: compact Person / Office / Interest / Loyalty / Action columns.
Marriage: a proposal view showing the two actual people/houses and readable
terms, expected obligations/costs, consequences and accept/decline actions.
Relations: Settlement / Ruling house / Faction / Agreement / Standing / Action.
Armies: commander, troops, riders/dragons, supply and readiness, then orders.
Records: reuse the existing structured tables/search/sort/filter/details,
including ruling-family and office badges. Do not regress to text walls.

USEFUL ACTIONS, AFTER THE UNDERLYING FEATURE EXISTS
View heir; Find spouse; Review proposal; Appoint office; Review city budget;
Feed dragon; View rider; Assign to army; Muster; Equip best; Buy missing kit.
Preview the real cost/affected people/items and revalidate before executing.
A brief button must not hide ownership, debt, loyalty or food consequences.

MOBILE DIRECTION
Keep touch targets at least the existing 48 px standard.
Phone dashboards stack compact cards; tables collapse into labelled rows with
the most important columns visible. Long records still have a details page.
Support tap rather than hover for tooltips, details and selection. Keep Back
and browsing state. Preserve desktop shortcuts without making them necessary.
Battle controls need clear pause/speed/deployment and troop/rider selection;
keep the same simulation underneath a touch-readable presentation.
Decide portrait/landscape handling with the first playable screen; do not lock
all future battles to a mockup's orientation.
No monetization, server or online-play model is specified by the title request.

EXTEND THE EXISTING THEME
game/ui/theme.gd owns shared theme colours/styles. WoodBg draws the original
wood pattern; it also needs deliberate treatment in the new game's copy.
Audit direct colours in Records, relations, charts, arrows, tabs and labels.
Changing only the theme constants can leave parchment/wood colours elsewhere.
Keep faction allegiance/map ownership readable independently of the new theme.
Do not recolour Copper Oath's installed release as part of this handoff.

BRAND
Working title: Burning Brood: Bloodlines.
Compact logo: BURNING BROOD, with BLOODLINES below.
Suggested tagline: Bleed or Burn.
Icon direction: a readable dragon/egg or dragon/crown silhouette, emerald
highlights and violet shadow, minimal text. An icon is future asset work.
