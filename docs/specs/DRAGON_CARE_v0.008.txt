BURNING BROOD: BLOODLINES - DRAGON CARE / GROWTH v0.008
Date: 2026-10-10 | Authored fictional-game calibration, not real animal biology.

ONE OUTCOME
Existing individual dragons/eggs receive real feed, shelter upkeep, condition
and bounded growth. No new people/units/accounts/markets/combat resolver.
Wyvern bodies, portraits, all v053 humanoid art and current gear remain retained.
No hatching, breeding, rider bonds, flight, breath or deployment this pass.

AUTHORITY / GOODS / COIN
Actual live owner person -> exact tracked current household after reconciliation.
Reject gone/cohort/estate/unclaimed/missing owners and unmatched household/house.
Keeper is an actual living person present at the saved location, outside away
military/company/travel service. Missing keeper interrupts automatic purchases.
Remote ownership is permitted: owner home purse -> saved location market till.
Fresh/preserved meat goods are native0.4kg portions, at actual posted retail
price x native TownEcon.markup. Feed demand=ceil(structural mass*.008/.4) units
per day, rounded whole portions. No grain/fruit substitution for carnivore feed.
One ration across both meat goods; substitute shortage demand is allocated once.
Wood shelter/warmth:1+floor(growth_points) bundles/day, egg1, maximum5.
Existing stock scarcity sharing limits a large request to quarter stock, except
small stock<=8 or sufficient stock>=4*request. No invented supplies/takers.
Exact TownEcon.pay_market transfer, stock decrement, used and native demand books.
No household-pool/store credit (would feed humans and dragons twice). No barn,
granary, private harvest claims, direct herd slaughter, treasury or company debit.

HUMAN RESERVES
Owner reserves cost of native seven-day essential-food gap, aging stored food
days since family.last without changing those stores. At least one coin remains.
Location market reserves three days' native household meal need after its
already-owned household pool; only remaining market food headroom can be bought.
Purse and supply limits apply sequentially to each dragon in stable ID order.
The pass does not promise balanced carrying capacity for every large dragon.

TIME / PERSISTENCE
World queues dragon_care after economy/families/army/sickness/herds and feudal.
Before charge, Feudal.finish reconciles lawful owner and present keeper.
Job day controls care guard, receipt and one-day growth, even if Game.day is a
later fractional/multi-day target. Never use elapsed final time to catch up
every prior day using today's prices, stocks or owner.
New records anchor last_day to current registration day after history finish.
Missing old care fields anchor to current day, with no retrospective billing;
cross-version migration remains unverified. Current load only normalizes fields.
Quotes, UI refresh, generic reconciliation, save/load and repeat/older jobs never
buy again. Only a newer explicit daily job buys at most one day's supplies.
Actual receipt stores payer, day, quantities/costs, feed/care ratios, condition
before/after, growth before/after, reason and per-good usage. One bounded latest
receipt per dragon; <=32 history entries only on shortage/recovery/maturity/death.

CONDITION / GROWTH
Health0..1. Daily change=.005*min(feed,care)-.018*(1-feed)-.005*(1-care).
Feed=actual portions/need; egg feed=1 with no food purchase. Shelter care=actual
wood/need, or0 when unavailable. Full care recovers .005/day; shortages lower
condition. At0 the existing record dies once, retains identity/bloodline/history,
and never pays again. No death creates a population or tactical-body casualty.
Growth points0..4 interpolate authored small/medium/large/huge/colossal physical
bases. Start at the original class exactly; no age/name/gem-derived rewrite.
Growth rate=feed*care*min(health_before,health_after)/(20*People.year_days()).
Requires feed>=.8,care>=.8,health>=.5. Native year120days; full band2400days.
Daily growth is bounded, no backdated age acceleration; maximumColossal4.
Juveniles become adult at native calendar age18; eggs never hatch this pass.
Linear interpolation changes structural mass/strength/linear tooth/claw size.
Original morph and independent seed multipliers apply once. Condition multiplies
strength by(.4+.6*health); size/force/bite/geometry remain separate from hardness.
Gem hardness and native tooth/claw material yields/strain/fracture stay unchanged.
Native body/battle binding remains the later v0.010 milestone.
