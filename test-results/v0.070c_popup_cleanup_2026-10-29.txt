ANIMATED POPUP INVENTORY
Scanned production game/**/*.gd, *.tscn and *.tres for tweens, animation players,
timers, deferred continuations, fade/slide and processing-driven transitions.
There are two animated overlay builders:
1. game/ui/kit.gd, UiKit.toast: immediate appearance, 2.2-second hold, then
   0.6-second fade-out and queue_free. Replacements also queue_free the old toast.
   Its existing p.create_tween() already binds animation to the popup. No leak
   reproduced during hold, fade-out, repeated close, host exit or six replacements.
2. game/battle/battle.gd, _banner: 0.5-second fade-in, caller-selected hold,
   0.8-second fade-out and queue_free. Used for initial/round battle titles,
   Aftermath, round results, Victory/Defeat/draw. Closing during fade-in left
   the tween alive on the longer-lived battle screen. Fixed by creating the
   tween on box instead, so Godot kills it when that popup leaves the tree.

UiKit.modal/picker/confirm/message, settings overlays, combat log, inspector,
readability legend, records and round-summary panels have no open/close animation.
The UiKit modal callers in ui/ and campaign/world_map.gd add no transition tween
or Timer. Sound's crossfade and actor/map/effect/portrait animation are unrelated
to popup opening/closing. No popup-specific Timer or AnimationPlayer was found.

REGRESSION
game/tests/test_popup_cleanup_v070.gd exercises the real toast and banner builders
without world generation or combat. It interrupts both banner fades, toast hold
and fade-out, closes twice, removes each host, and replaces toasts six times.
It also closes modal/picker/confirm/message/settings through their close paths.
It compares Performance.OBJECT_NODE_COUNT and OBJECT_ORPHAN_NODE_COUNT, exact
live node IDs, Timer IDs, processing node IDs and signal connection counts to
baseline. Owned popup descendants must be freed, all remaining signal callables
valid, and get_processed_tweens() empty (including paused tweens).
Tween.custom_step crosses long holds without real-time waits or new timers;
each interruption still advances real process frames before/after close.
No RenderingServer.frame_post_draw wait is used, including in headless mode.
Registered block: popup_cleanup_v070 (new regression plus test_ui_modal.gd).
Existing runner already supports both block registration and --tests paths.
