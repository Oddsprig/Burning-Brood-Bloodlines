TOWN SCREEN SPEC PART 2 of 3 (Register to Hardship). Same rules and frame as part 1 (every number from the sim; sort any column; tap any name -> person page).

5. REGISTER (Douglas 23:42, 00:48)
- Header: people, families, children/adults/elders, female/male, homeless, empty homes, sick.
- Filters: class (poor / middling / rich), state, kind (family / institution), homeless only, for hire only, in debt only, surname search. Row: family, home address, class, state, people, purse, income/day, HUNGER % (share of the last 14 days the family went short of food) and CONDITION (Douglas 01:27): comfortable / getting by / scraping by / hungry / cold / sick at home ("2 sick") / in mourning / destitute, the worst real one shown, coloured green to red. Sort by hunger puts the hungriest first; a purse below 0 (owes more than it has) shows in red.
- Family page: condition and hunger at the top; hunger 14 days %, cold 14 days %, winter clothing %, sick today, pawned goods (value), mourning until day N (after a death: work and spending slowed for some days [ours]), rent a month and payments missed; purse, income today, spent today, spent on food today, food in store by kind ("grain 20 days: barley 8, rye 12"), SEED KEPT for next sowing (farm families), drink days, firewood days, clothing, debt total, home (owns / tenant of X family, rent a month / homeless), possessions, land acres by crop, herds, businesses owned.
- MEMBERS: every person, eldest first. Line 1: name, age, sex, then OCCUPATION and WAGE A DAY right-aligned next to the name. Line 2: employer or roster, or home work for those who keep house ("spinning, 10 a day": piece work for a business, real wage) (Gate Mill / garrison / caravan crew / servant to the X household / player's company), "for hire: <reason>" if in the recruit pool, sickness if sick. Tap -> person page: life history (jobs held, big events), skills from work, rosters he is on.
- Loans: lender, principal, owed, rate, pay per month, missed, collateral.
- Life: family history lines, newest first.

6. LAND & HOMES (Douglas 00:46)
- Header: total acres owned by all residents, cultivated by crop, pasture, WOODLAND (NEW SIM: woodcutters fell real woodland acres; slow regrowth), herds by animal; acres held by the state, acres for sale.
- By owner: families sorted by acres (tap).
- Map: see 07-land-map.md (square-tile plot map, tap any plot).
- Homes: count, empty, seized, for sale, rent this month, value index (x1.00 = build price). Row: home, kind (per race), owner, occupants, condition, value, rent, mortgage/loan, for sale. Filters: empty, for sale.

7. CENSUS (Douglas 23:42)
- Chart: population at each season's census.
- Table, one row per season: people, families, children, adults, elders, female, male, births, deaths, homeless, sick, soldiers, empty homes. Tap a row -> that season's deaths by cause.
- WHAT IS KILLING PEOPLE (Douglas 02:50: the census must show what causes the deaths).
  - Every death records ONE real cause, set by the system that killed him: battle (which fight), wounds later, starved, sickness (which disease), frozen, old age, childbirth, executed, accident. Never blank or "unknown"; a missing cause fails the test.
  - Card "Births and deaths": births vs deaths chart (per season, plus the 60-day daily buffer), and a row per cause with count this season, last season and per 1,000 people. Tap a cause -> the dead: name, age, sex, family, job, date, where (tap -> person page; the dead stay findable in the register).
  - Card "Coming and going": left (to which town, why: hunger, debt, war, work, married away, called up) and arrived (from where, why).
  - Balance line: births - deaths + arrived - left = change in people since last census. It must add up.

8. TREASURY (reference Treasury + Purse income)
- Cash, chart, in/out today, taxes and rates, arrears, loans out; TRIBUTE owed up to the faction this month and paid so far; relief now (poor relief / soup kitchen open or closed, from the head's rule); clerks and tax collectors paid or unpaid; the company's own dues here (tolls, fees paid).
- Accounts: this vs last month, every real flow (taxes, fines, tolls, garrison and militia pay, armory, granary, poorhouse, loan interest, seized sales, loot share).
- LOANS REGISTER (Douglas 01:11): every loan the town has made. Header: count and total out. Row: #, borrower (tap), kind (family loan / business loan / mortgage / tax arrears), principal, owed, rate, pay/month, missed, status (current / late in red / defaulted / repaid), collateral (tap home or land), clerk who made it (a real person). Filter: all / late / defaulted. Defaults feed the forced-sale loop.
- Ledger: last 60 entries.

9. HARDSHIP (reference Pressure)
- Hunger %, homeless, destitute, indebted, poorhouse inmates, labour wanted vs offered, day wage, food price index, with 60-day charts.
- Desperate list: the recruit pool as the sim ranks it, with reasons.
- Seizures and forced sales this season.
- Coming and going: families that left this year and where to (hunger, war, seizure), refugees arrived and from where, people in the poorhouse, sleeping rough. NEW SIM if hungry families currently just vanish: they must walk to a real town and join its register.
