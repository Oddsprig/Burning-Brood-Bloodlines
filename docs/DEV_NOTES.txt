Shipped player war, through 0.094:

- Raising is a reviewed quote. `Mobilization.muster` moves real people, kit and meal stock, and pays the family purse from army coin. The muster spinner stops at 200. The code ceiling is `Mobilization.LIMIT` 1000. Rounds still field 100 fit fighters a side, up to 20 rounds. A loss sends living player troops home for 5 remuster days (`BattleRounds.retreat_player`) and a beaten people-band home (`retreat_band`).
- The player's own battle uses `BattleSim` on the current round's Units. Pools hold the same Unit objects, so wounds stay on the body between rounds. Dragons are not captured. Their downs and rider seats accumulate (`dragon_downs`, `DragonCombat.merge_seats`) and `final_result` hands that record to `DragonCombat.on_battle_result`. A rider with no company Unit keeps a body in the dragon registry (`rider_bodies`) and heals one care day at a time. Home defence admits only a healthy adult at its owning settlement with the bonded adult present. March writes `campaign_march` and `DragonCare.settle` draws named meat on later mornings. Autobattle stays on the ground; `flying` is stored and does not add flight.
- Raid and siege are real settlement actions. `Warfare.preview` refuses an own town, `Diplomacy.peace_bound`, an empty army, distance, a current encounter, and a raid inside 30 days. `start` calls that preview before reputation and the battle. An empty garrison resolves plunder or conquest immediately. A won raid takes 35% of treasury and 12% of ordinary household purses into army `gold`, burns 40% of grain, and kills about 3% of anonymous cohort slots. A won siege transfers the town. v0.092's tested player guard is this refusal; the area diff only made the sentence name a truce. The predicate is in campaign/diplomacy.gd and includes active truce.
- Off-screen faction battles and militia sallies already exist. They resolve by power and round counts, then delete or mark real participants. They do not run tissue combat. An AI dragon on `ai_march` can be marked dead by that roll (`take_clash_loss`).
- Creatures stay off unless the loaded world sets `creatures_enabled`. `start_battle` returns before borrowing dragons if a suspended creature roster is in the fight.
- v0.091 equipment shading is in `FigurePainter.metal` and the shield pass. Mounted and identity checks are accepted by the 0.091 receipt. This audit did not redraw.
- v0.093 accepted measurement, not re-run here: 108 native multi-engagement battles, 306 engagements, roster/wound/rider continuity kept. Small Ruby adults won 0/12 and large adults 9/12. Broad balance stays unresolved. There is no dragon purchase price. Authored size strength is 1000 / 1150 / 1300 / 1500 / 1750 against mass 350 / 1200 / 5000 / 18000 / 65000 kg (`game/data/dragons.json`).
