START
Use Burning Brood Bloodlines v0.022 on your actual Desktop (green dragon/crown).
It opens Desktop/Burning_Brood_Bloodlines_v0.022. For another extracted copy,
run Install_Desktop.cmd, or Launch_Bloodlines.cmd with game/ and runtime/ beside
it. Official Windows64 Godot4.7.2 is bundled; first launch prepares quietly.
Use a fresh Normal world with1 history day. Select a settlement on the shown map,
choose one of its real local houses, customize, review and press Begin.
Saves use the separate Bloodlines user-data folder; tests use private paths.

THIS VERSION
Each living dragon keeps one saved physical body. At its actual home settlement,
review Defend this settlement to enable a healthy adult with a present, real
bonded rider. Defence starts OFF. A qualifying local battle adds that same dragon
alongside your army; it moves and attacks automatically. Withdraw stops future
participation. Being away never summons or moves it.

Bite/claw combat uses the existing material, tissue, armour, size, strength,
geometry, wound, blood and death calculations. Gem hardness applies to teeth and
claws; scales and skin remain organic. Permanent injuries stay on the same body
after battle/save/load. Native ground sprites retain coherent proportions and
show blood/mangling; head separation removes the head at the neck.

RETAINED v021 RIDER BONDS
House -> Dragons: visit an owned living dragon's settlement and review an actual
local adult before confirming its rider bond. Your current household leader can
manage dragons owned by living members of that exact household. Each person and
dragon has at most one rider bond. The rider portrait and record use that actual
person. Release requires an explicit review; a death ends the bond once, while
travel alone preserves it. Ownership, custody and location stay distinct.
No people, equipment or army funds are created by a bond. Mounted-character
battle display and targeting are23; breath is24; campaign following/AI is25.

RETAINED v020 SALVAGE
Open Loot on the compact battle results screen to see each actual salvage buyer:
settlement, market business, owning household, price, paid coins and destination.
The nearest economy market still buys automatically. Its actual till pays the
Army purse; only fully paid pieces move into its stock or inventory. Pieces it
cannot afford remain in your stash with their exact condition and identity.
Metal transfers preserve whole kilograms and counted fractional residue. Battle
coin, camp coin, gear sales and retained equipment are shown separately.
AI field loot and camp hoards use the same accountable native sale path.

RETAINED v019 SELECTION
The world map stays visible through settlement selection, local-house selection,
Customize House and review. Click a settlement marker or its list row; the actual
location is highlighted. Compact8-row pages/search keep the lists manageable.
Choose the ruling family or another tracked local household. The actual leader,
portrait, household purse and role are shown. Population cohorts are not houses.

Customize the displayed house name, one of8 emblems, and banner colours. Review
before Begin; Back and all previews leave the world and its people/funds untouched.
Begin selects the reviewed real person/household and positions the existing party
at that settlement. Your name/banner are saved and appear in House and on the map.
Native household identities, ownership and family names remain unchanged.

Ruling houses retain their actual office/authority. Ordinary households keep
their own property and purse; they do not receive town lordship, settlement
taxes, public forces, armory or treasury control. A shared dynasty name does not
grant those rights. No field army or army money is created on selection.
House -> Laws shows your actual house leader and real successor, including
ordinary households; settlement office succession remains its existing system.

RETAINED18 RECORDS / FONTS / LAWS
City records now group navigation under Overview, People, Economy and Land &
defence. Select a category, then its relevant tab. Real Overview/Register/
Businesses/Census/Treasury/Garrison data stays aligned and paged; open rows for
actual owner/person detail, with native search/sort and return context intact.
Compact dark rows replace invisible cream-on-cream text. Regular and bold
Liberation Sans fonts are bundled with the complete SIL OFL license.

House -> Laws shows the current native race-specific rule, ruler, eligible heir
and recorded regent when applicable. Person links open the actual registry and
return to Laws. Laws are read-only; this release changes presentation. Inline
succession information is removed from the current city/house chooser. The new
settlement/map/local-house/Customize workflow is now available.

RETAINED v017 ARMY FOUNDATION
A new campaign and house selection leave your private field army UNRAISED.
There are no free100 people,75000 army coins, invented equipment or provisions.
Public city guards and militia are separate real forces. Your ruler portrait
remains near the map's lower left; Army opens the new army management screen.

At your actual home settlement, fund the army from your house's real private
purse. Review how many ADDITIONAL people can depart, actual available equipment,
food/signing costs and three days of total field wages. Confirm the review to
raise those real people. A quote/cancel spends nothing. Capacity is200 TOTAL,
with up to8 real officers. A shortage gives an honest partial muster and receipt.
No automatic transfer from house, city or realm treasuries occurs.

Return to the actual home settlement and stand down. The same people, bodies,
wounds, family identities and private equipment stay recorded at home. Borrowed
city kit returns to its actual armory; living residents rejoin civilian work
once and stop drawing field wages. Re-raising retains their physical identity.
Dismissal follows the same home return. Native aging, sickness and death still
apply. Private equipment of natural deaths remains in saved household-linked
estate custody; management of those estates is a later action, not a market sale.
Remote records cannot move your company home or authorize a remote return.

RETAINED GAMEPLAY
House/army/city/realm funds have separate owners. Businesses/land/herds belong
to tracked families; common folk provide most work and their pooled wealth pays
real local taxes and tribute. Native material/tissue/body/wound/blood battles,
100-200-person forces, feudal allegiance and defection remain authoritative.
Automatic battle slow motion defaults OFF (Esc/Settings -> Gameplay to enable).
Paused/end Combat log records the full fight, with18-row pages, character filters
and native damage details. Results show compact casualties and expandable detail.
Standing fighting officers rally assigned fleeing followers on BOTH sides;
death/incapacity/withdrawal removes support. Reading logs never reruns combat.
The right performance panel shows frame/FPS, calculation times, jobs and memory.
Army interface cost and Army management stage are now included.

Copper Oath v0.053 released humanoid races, genders, hair/beards, weapons and
armour remain exact. Dragons/eggs keep individual gem types, owners, care and
growth; organic sprites have gem-coloured horns/teeth/claws. Head/jaw movement,
blood/mangling and saddle handles remain. Real rider bonds are now recorded;
native dragon battle bodies are active in22, mounted-character presentation in23.
Background monsters remain suspended. Bronze smiths recycle counted inputs.
