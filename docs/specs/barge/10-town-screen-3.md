TOWN SCREEN SPEC PART 3 of 3 (Garrison, Roads, Books, tests). Same rules and frame as part 1 (every number from the sim; sort any column; tap any name -> person page).

10. GARRISON (one roster per force based here: garrison, militia)
- Men paid for, pay bill a day, days of pay left, unpaid days, morale, kit %, drill %, war footing yes/no, deserted this year; this month: promotion tests, passed, promoted; sleeping in barracks vs at home.
- Ranks and posts in the culture's names: filled of cap, pay per rank as "base + N a grade".
- PAY BY RANK AND WEAPON GRADE (Douglas 01:47): pay = rank base + a small amount per grade in the man's own weapon skill. Grade = his REAL skill level with that weapon (existing skill XP from fights, plus drill), the same ladder up to Legendary. Rank and grade are separate: a Legendary swordsman can still be a plain footman and is paid footman base + his Legendary grade. Only the weapon he serves with counts for pay; other skills do not (Douglas 01:48). Every race, in its own pay form (Douglas 01:49): a blooded orc who is a Legendary axeman gets blooded base plus his skill step, paid in bronze cash like every race (Douglas 01:51, see 05-coinage.md). Culture row sets rank base and the step per skill level [ours: ~5% of base]. (Barge part 3 section 3 fixes the step at 5 coins a grade.) A grade-up raises his pay the next day and is logged in his life history and the Books.
- Weapons + avg quality; armory vs target.
- Roster row: rank + name, family, age, weapon, weapon skill level, XP to next level, years served, pay a day (base + grade), kill renown. Tap -> person page.

11. ROADS (reference River)
- Routes to neighbours: days, danger from real sightings (raids, war bands, monsters).
- Caravans owned here: route, cargo, crew, status; this year sent, arrived, robbed.
- Arms contracts, trade partners; road log, last 20 events.

11b. BOOKS (Douglas 01:25: a log book of what people are doing business-wise; reference town "Log")
- "What the books noted, latest first": one plain sentence per real business event in this town, built from live values, never stock text. Kinds: harvest buying ("The barley is being cut. The Harrows' mill pays 26 the sack at the fields, 47 in the town."), shipments ("Wat the tanner sends 35 pieces of leather to Northwatch on the Harrows' wagons, 51 a piece to him after the freight."), caravans leaving and arriving, hires and quits, loans taken and defaults, seizures and forced sales, businesses opened and closed, price swings with their reason.
- Year-end line per business: inputs used, goods made, sold in town vs sent away, coins earned.
- Filter by business, family, kind; tap a name -> business, family or person. Each business and family page shows its own slice.

- Every Books line's coins and counts match the ledger and stock moves of that day.
