v0.099 - Release candidate: dragons in big battles, store builds, marriage ages
Normal merge: 1fcb3fe758ad3a566cbb6b4466b25706fc3cd1d0 (branch v0.099-release-candidate).
Base 7ad5a8b (main after v0.098 notes).
Big battles: in a battle of rounds your fit bonded dragons now fight every
round. Before, the field took the first 100 a side in pool order and dragons
are listed after every man, so with 100+ men a dragon never fought. Men still
rotate (rested before tired) and a dragon too hurt to fight still sits out.
The enemy's dragons follow the same rule.
Marriage: an age gap is weighed in the longer-lived partner's years (adult
life adult..elder: men 39 years, elves 260, dwarves 95). Elf and dwarf houses
had almost no one to marry (an elf of 164 had no matches; now 19). Men, orcs
and goblins keep exactly the old 16-year limit and score.
Battle inspector: "(80% done)" and "Speed: steady   Morale 70" instead of
"(12/15 ticks)" and "Speed 944 (lower is faster) tempo x1.04".
Muster: when no family member can ride, it says who is away serving (the
realm's field army, the town guard, a caravan).
Store build: the shown name is "Burning Brood: Bloodlines" (title tagline,
window title, app name; the save folder name is unchanged, so saves still
load). New Windows Desktop export preset (one 64-bit .exe with the game inside,
red dragon icon, publisher Oddsprig, version 0.99.0.0). Android preset 0.099
(code 99) with launcher icons, a monochrome crown for themed icons and the boot
splash that project.godot named but was never committed (icon_mobile/).
tools/build_release.sh rebuilds both; the Android release key is passed by
environment and is never in Git.
Tests: new tests/test_release_v099.gd (33 checks, 0 failures; fails 2 on the
old dragon code). Nine older tests updated to current rules (all failed the
same way on main): test_battle_screen, claude_prisoners_v033,

test_art_paperdoll. test_ui_records_v044 splits into four runs under 3 minutes
(--part=world|faction|wide|tall).

v0.097 - Six bug fixes toward an Oddsprig sale
Normal merge: 7b0429ab4aa596498624f862398c4bcd30e6b737 (branch v0.097-sale-readiness).
Base 623d009 (main after v0.096 notes).
Weddings: a betrothed pair weds only when BOTH are of age; a child is no longer
wed because the other partner came of age. Ages follow the household's own race
(an elf of 20 in a human town is a child). Betrothal texts say so. A town no
longer marries off a betrothed person while they wait; a betrothal ends if one
of the pair married another. A spouse who just came of age is not left behind
as a duplicate in their old family.
Battle loot: after a battle of rounds only the dead are stripped; men who fled
or left alive with their beaten army keep their purse and kit
(BattleRounds "enemy_withdrew", Loot.left_the_field). A man who fled early and
died later is still looted. Your own men who fled now show as retreats in the
aftermath of a battle of rounds.
Hurt dragons: a dragon that "cannot fight" (Health report) sits out later
rounds like a man, and its rider stays out with it instead of fighting on
foot; a rider who cannot fight is not seated, so the dragon fights riderless.
House improvements: every material must be in the market in full; the reason
names what is short; the build re-checks stock and takes nothing if short.
Court lists: marriage matches are no longer cut at 24; matches (24 a page) and
prisoners (40 a page) have a "Show N more (M not shown)" button.
Records text: Carriers names pack donkeys carrying return loads (counting only
caravans whose donkeys are alive); Research says
trials burn iron bars, flux stone and charcoal (not wood).
